Accessibility statement

Last updated July 2026

The Stakeholder's Family Office wants every visitor to be able to read this site, operate it, and book a call, whatever tools they browse with. This page says what standard we build to, how we test it, and how to reach us if something is in your way.

The standard

This website is designed and tested to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

What that means in practice

Every part of the site works with a keyboard alone, and the element you are on always shows a visible focus outline. The page keeps its full content and function at small screens, at 400 percent zoom, and with user-adjusted text spacing. Animation respects your reduced-motion setting, and the site remains fully readable with JavaScript turned off. Headings, landmarks, and form labels are marked up so screen readers can navigate by structure.

How we test

We run an automated accessibility check (axe-core, WCAG 2.x A and AA rules) plus a manual keyboard walk, a 320 pixel reflow check, and a text-spacing check before changes ship, and the same battery after deployment. This statement reflects testing completed in July 2026.

If something is not working for you

If any part of this site is hard to read or operate, tell us and we will fix it or provide the information another way. Write to info@eg-capital.co. We reply within one business day.
